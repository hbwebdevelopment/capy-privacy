Privacy Policy

Capy Virtual Pet · Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version: We do not collect any personal information from you or your child, and there are no accounts or sign-ups. Capy Virtual Pet shows optional, child-safe adverts from Google AdMob, which players can choose to watch to unlock items. To show these adverts, Google collects limited technical information about the device. It is not used to build a profile of your child or show personalised adverts.

Who we are

Capy Virtual Pet is made by HB Web Development, run by Hayley Bevan in Kent, United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at info@hbwebdevelopment.co.uk.

Made for children

Capy Virtual Pet is designed for children and families. We have built it so that children can play safely without sharing any information about themselves. We encourage parents and guardians to read this policy and talk to their children about it.

Information we collect

We (HB Web Development) do not collect any personal information. The app does not:

  • ask for names, email addresses, ages or any other personal details
  • require an account or login
  • use our own analytics or tracking tools
  • access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos

Adverts

Capy Virtual Pet is free to play and is supported by adverts provided by Google AdMob. We have set up our adverts to be suitable for children:

  • Adverts only play when a player chooses to watch one, for example to unlock an item. They are never forced.
  • The app tells Google that it is directed at children, so Google does not show personalised or interest-based adverts and does not use the information for behavioural advertising.
  • Adverts are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences, and we block sensitive categories such as gambling and dating.
  • The app does not use the device's advertising ID.

What Google AdMob collects

To show adverts, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's advertising software collects some technical information automatically when an advert is shown. This may include:

  • the device's IP address, which can indicate a rough location such as the country
  • basic device information, such as the device model and Android version
  • interactions with adverts, such as whether an advert was viewed or tapped
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance and crash data related to adverts
  • app-specific identifiers used for fraud prevention and limiting how often adverts are shown

This information is sent securely (encrypted) to Google and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can read more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive any personal information about you or your child from Google.

Internet access

The app uses the internet only to load adverts. The rest of the game works without an internet connection. If you would prefer no adverts to load, you can turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data while playing.

Game progress

Your capy's progress (such as their name, happiness and items) is saved only on your own device so you can carry on playing next time. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, this saved progress is deleted.

Sharing information

We do not collect, share, sell or pass on any personal information. The only information that leaves the device is the technical information collected by Google AdMob, described above, in order to show adverts.

Google Play

Capy Virtual Pet is downloaded through Google Play. Google may collect some information when you use Google Play, for example to handle downloads and updates. This is covered by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one.

Your rights

We follow UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code), as well as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). As we do not hold any personal information about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information collected by Google AdMob, you can contact Google using the details in their Privacy Policy. If you have any concerns, please contact us and we will be happy to help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Capy Virtual Pet works, we will update this policy before the change goes live and change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
